# Open positions

We are seeking PhD and MS students passionate about developing computational algorithms for genome analysis. Our potential projects include genome assembly, read alignment, variant calling, pangenome analysis, and other related research in cancer and plant genomes. If you are interested, please [email your CV](mailto:haoyu.cheng@yale.edu) to further explore these opportunities.

## Prospective students 
If you are interested in working with us, please apply through Yale graduate programs: Computational Biology and Bioinformatics ([CBB](https://cbb.yale.edu/) - PhD & MS). We also welcome visiting students; feel free to contact me directly via email.
